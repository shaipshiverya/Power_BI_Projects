## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard built on the 
Sample Superstore dataset to analyze sales 
performance, profitability, and regional trends 
across the United States.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Executive Summary
| KPI | Value |
|-----|-------|
| Total Sales | $2M |
| Total Profit | $286K |
| Profit Margin | 12.5% |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |
| YoY Growth | 46.9% |

**Visuals included:**
- Total Sales per Month (vs Last Year)
- Total Sales by Category
- YoY Growth % by Segment

### 2️⃣ Regional Breakdown
- Sales vs Profitability by Sub-Category (Bubble Chart)
- Region-wise Sales & Profit Margin by Category
- US State Map

## 🔧 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard & Visualizations

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- 📈 Technology is the highest revenue category ($0.84M)
- 📉 Furniture has the lowest profit margin (1.4%)
- 🌍 West region leads in total sales ($2.51M)
- 📅 Sales peak in November–December (holiday season)
- 🏢 Corporate segment shows 52.1% YoY growth

---

## 🗂️ Dataset
- **Source:** Sample Superstore (Kaggle / Tableau Public)
- **Records:** ~10,000 orders
- **Fields:** Order ID, Category, Region, Sales, 
  Profit, Quantity, Discount, Segment, Ship Mode
